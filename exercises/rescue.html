Sligo Animal Rescue

Helping Animals Find Safe and Loving Homes

Animal Rescue

We provide care and support for animals in Sligo that have been abandoned, lost or are in need of a safe place to stay.

Our dedicated volunteers work to give every rescued animal food, shelter, veterinary care and plenty of attention.

Animal Rehoming

We help suitable cats, dogs and other animals find caring and responsible new families.

Our rehoming service works with potential adopters to help match each animal with a safe and suitable home.

Community Support

We provide information and support to local pet owners to encourage responsible animal care throughout Sligo.

Our community activities also help raise awareness about animal welfare, adoption and the importance of responsible pet ownership.
