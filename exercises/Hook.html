Hook Head Lighthouse

Discover Ireland's Historic Coastal Landmark

Lighthouse Tours

Explore the historic Hook Head Lighthouse and learn about its long maritime history during a guided visitor tour.

Discover how the lighthouse helped guide ships safely along the rugged coast of County Waterford.

Coastal Views

Enjoy spectacular views of the Irish coastline from Hook Head and experience the dramatic scenery surrounding the lighthouse.

The rocky landscape and Atlantic Ocean provide a memorable setting for photography, sightseeing and peaceful walks.

Visitor Experience

Hook Head offers visitors an opportunity to explore a unique part of Ireland's heritage while enjoying the surrounding coastal landscape.

Families, history enthusiasts and visitors looking for a scenic day out can all discover something special at this historic location.
